Where it all started...

Edinburgh has always been the heart and soul of this business. Having grown up in the city, I thought it was only right for me to open my business here as well.

From a young age I have been baking cakes and fancy treats, however, only in the last few years I decided to quit my full-time job and go all in with the business.
